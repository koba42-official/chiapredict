How It Works
Four Steps to Trustless Prediction
From market creation to settlement — everything happens on-chain.
1

Mint Market

A Rue smart contract mints equal quantities of YES and NO CAT tokens, locked to a resolution condition.

2

Trade on Dexie

YES and NO tokens are freely tradeable on Dexie DEX. Prices emerge from supply and demand — a live probability.

3

Oracle Resolves

When the event occurs, a designated oracle submits the outcome on-chain, triggering the settlement puzzle.

4

Redeem for XCH

Winning tokens are redeemable 1:1 for XCH. Losing tokens become worthless. Fully trustless settlement.

Technology
Built Different
Chia's coin-set (UTXO) model enables prediction markets with properties impossible on account-based chains.
⚡

Rue Smart Contracts

Market logic compiled in Rue, Chia's high-level language. Deterministic, auditable, and composable puzzle programs.

🔗

Coin-Set (UTXO) Model

No global state, no reentrancy, no MEV. Each market is an independent coin with self-contained settlement logic.

🔐

Trustless Settlement

Oracle signatures are validated by the puzzle itself. No admin keys, no multisig, no escape hatches. Code is law.
